ATP Top 100 · Player Film

Design & Build

Designed and built by Carlo De Marchis — “A guy with a scarf”. The concept, the ranking-ordered card-film layout, the visual language and the interactions are my own — an alternative, more playful way to move through a season of ATP data, told in my own style.

Data & Images — atptour.com

Every number and every image here comes from the official ATP site, atptour.com: the PIF ATP Rankings and Race to Turin, each player’s season activity and match-by-match results, and the player imagery (round headshots and full-body cutouts). All of it is the property of the ATP and its partners — it is used here only to power this fan experience, and the credit belongs entirely to them.

Built With

Designed and developed with Claude (Anthropic) — Claude Code for the build — and hosted on GitHub Pages. A single self-contained page, vanilla JavaScript.

A Note & A Disclaimer

This is an independent, non-commercial fan project. I made it purely for the love of tennis, of data-visualisation, and of interactive fan experiences on digital — as a way to show that there are alternative ways to create engaging experiences on top of data and visuals that already exist, in one’s own style. I have no commercial intent whatsoever. Everything shown is the property of the ATP and the respective rights-holders, used with respect and gratitude. If you hold the rights to anything here and would rather it wasn’t shown, just get in touch and I’ll take it down right away.

Made with love. — Carlo De Marchis, “A guy with a scarf”
