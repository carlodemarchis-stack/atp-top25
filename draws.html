How to read the draws

The 2026 ATP Grand Slams & Masters 1000 — one radial fan per event, first round on the rim, champion at the hub.
  • SWITCHChange tournament with the ‹ › arrows, a pip, or the ← → keys. Gold pips are Grand Slams, blue are Masters 1000, in calendar order.
  • COLOUREach player keeps one colour from their first match until they lose. The champion's run runs gold all the way to the centre.
  • PLAYERClick any name — or press ↑ ↓ to step through the finishing order. Their run lights up; the panels show photo, seed, result, who they lost to, W–L for matches / sets / games, and every match played.
  • MATCHHover a dot for the scoreline; click a dot to pin that box open.
  • RESETClick empty space to clear the selection and return to the champion.
